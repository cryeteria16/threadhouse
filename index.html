About us

Thread House Trading is a tailoring supplies shop in Al Nahda, Sharjah. We stock the materials and accessories that tailors, boutiques and home sewists rely on every day, from quality threads and zippers to linings, interfacings, buttons and tools.

Whether you're stitching a single dress or supplying a full workshop, we'll help you find the right trims at the right quantity.

  • Wide range Threads, zips, buttons, elastic, linings and more under one roof.
  • Trade & retail Buy a single piece or stock up in bulk.
  • Trusted brands Including YKK zips and Kohinoor press buttons.
